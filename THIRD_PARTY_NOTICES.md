## awesome-design-md

`prompts/style-packs.json` contains color palettes and mood words derived from
DESIGN.md files in [VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md).
Only colors and mood are reused; typography, spacing, components, and grid
rules are not included.

Source files used:

- `design-md/claude`
- `design-md/linear.app`
- `design-md/stripe`
- `design-md/spotify`
- `design-md/apple`
